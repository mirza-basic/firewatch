Alert SMS reference

FireWatch Alert SMS

Every alert carries the map link and fits one 160-character segment. These are rendered from your real event data, not mocked up.

What lands on the phone

Four lines, or five once an active fire has weather attached. The bar under each message is the character budget against the 160-character segment limit.

The test message

Sent by the test-sms workflow. Same body as a real alert so it exercises the same wording and segment cost — but it leads with TEST, because a drill that reads like a fire on someone's phone at 3am is worse than no drill.

First line, by what changed

Only the first four send an SMS. UGASEN and POTVRDJEN are informational and would cost a segment to say a fire cooled off.

When a place name will not fit

One settlement in your data is called Ekološko izletište „Ontario”. Rather than spill into a second segment, the message drops the distance and bearing, then trims the name at a word boundary.

Two judgement calls worth your eye

1.9/1.9MW means peak first, current second — ten characters cheaper than 1.9MW maks/1.9 sada, but not self-evident on a first read. And there is no IZVAN OPĆINE marker, so a fire in the 2 km band reads exactly like one inside the municipality.
