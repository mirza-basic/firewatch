Alert Telegram reference

FireWatch Alert Telegram

One public channel, one post per alert. Unlike the SMS version there is no 160-character segment to fit inside — the limit is 4096 — so nothing here is trimmed, folded to ASCII, or left out for cost. These are rendered from your real event data, not mocked up.

What lands in the channel

Five lines once an active fire has weather attached, four without it — one more than SMS ever sends, because the detection count, its source list and the footprint width fit here with room to spare.

The test message

Sent by the test-telegram workflow. Same body shape as a real alert so it exercises the same wording — but it leads with TEST, because a drill that reads like a fire in the channel is worse than no drill.

One thing this format does not do

There is no worst_case() here and nothing degrades — a long settlement name or a long FIREWATCH_PUBLIC_URL just makes the post a little longer, nowhere near 4096 characters. The one limit that is real is Telegram's own: about one post per second to the channel. A 429 response is retried once, after waiting what Telegram itself asks for (retry_after, capped at 5 s) — past that cap the post is dropped rather than blocking the next poll cycle on it.
